About us

Our Story

House of Dimpho started in a garage with limited resources but with a strong passion for beauty and a desire to build something meaningful.

As more clients discovered our services, the business continued to grow. This allowed us to move from a small garage based business into a bigger space where we could serve more clients.

Our journey continues as we work towards growing the business while maintaining good customer service and quality beauty care.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide quality beauty and hair care services that help our clients feel confident, comfortable and beautiful.

Our Vision

Our mission is to grow House Of Dimpho into a trusted business that is recognised for quality service and customer satisfaction

Our Values

  • Quality
  • Professionalism
  • Respect
  • Customer Satisfaction
  • Creativity
